# Business problem

## Client scenario
A fictional European wholesale distributor sells products across five European markets and serves SMB, Mid-Market and Enterprise customers. Management needs a consistent view of commercial performance.

## Questions to answer
1. Which countries and product categories contribute the most net revenue?
2. How are revenue and gross profit trending month by month?
3. Which customer segments are most valuable?
4. What is the gross margin by product category and segment?
5. Which customers account for the largest share of revenue?

## Scope and limitations
This is an independent portfolio simulation using entirely synthetic data. It does not represent Civitta client data, confidential OQEMA data, or actual company performance. The generated dataset covers calendar year 2025 only.
