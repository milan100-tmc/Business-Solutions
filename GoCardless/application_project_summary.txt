Independent portfolio project: Payment Collection Performance Analysis

I built a synthetic payment-attempt dataset (8,000 payments, 8,576 attempts) and analysed it in SQL (tested in DuckDB), Excel PivotTables and an interactive dashboard to show how I would help an operations team reduce failed collections. The work separates first-attempt success from final success, measures retry recovery and coverage, and analyses failure reasons and merchant segments by count and by value (about EUR 33k fails at first attempt; about EUR 20k remains uncollected after retries). I validated the data with quality checks and reconciliations, cross-checked results in a second tool, tested whether differences were larger than noise, and wrote a one-page handover memo with definitions and limitations. The data is synthetic and the project is independent; it is not based on GoCardless data. In your stack I would adapt the modelling to dbt and the dashboard to Looker or DOMO.
